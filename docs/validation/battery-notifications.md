# Battery notifications validation — 2026-10-30

Implemented local tray balloons using the existing Engine::batteries readback. No transport, stock service, device write, driver or profile changes. Existing global low-warning switch remains the master switch. Wireless devices in native Settings gain independent enable, 1–95% threshold, and opt-in charging-start/stop controls. Preferences live in tray.json battery_devices by existing device key; unknown preference fields survive edits.

Low alerts occur once per discharge episode, reset by confirmed charging or ten percentage points of recovery. Unknown charging and missing/disconnected samples retain the low latch, break transition continuity and never imply charging stopped. First sample/reconnect establishes a charging baseline silently. Muting consumes a low episode to avoid replay on unmute. Suppression lasts for the tray process; restarting can warn again. Device identity follows existing keys and inherits their identity limitations. Missing battery readback is labelled unknown/unavailable in the tooltip; absent devices disappear from connected-device UI rather than appearing as zero percent. No disconnected toast is generated.
